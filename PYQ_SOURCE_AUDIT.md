## 1. ABSOLUTE ZERO-FABRICATION & PROVENANCE POLICY

In strict accordance with the **SIH26043** guidelines and **Prompt 4**:
* **NEVER INVENT A QUESTION**: Every question in the verified registry is an authentic historical item from a real university examination paper.
* **NEVER PRESENT AI GENERATION AS A HISTORICAL PYQ**: AI models are strictly prohibited from writing or reconstructing past university exam questions.
* **NEVER MERGE UNVERIFIED QUESTIONS INTO OFFICIAL REPOSITORIES**: Any question whose official university paper provenance cannot be established is categorized as `UNVERIFIED` or `DEMO`.
* **MANDATORY COVID & DISRUPTION TRANSPARENCY**: Where sessions were disrupted, canceled, or converted to internal assignments, they are recorded as `NOT_AVAILABLE` rather than filled with hallucinated questions.
* **CATEGORICAL VERIFICATION STATUSES**:
  1. `VERIFIED`: Directly sourced and verified against official university examination branch archives, BoS papers, or gazettes.
  2. `PARTIALLY_VERIFIED`: Confirmed via affiliated institutional digital libraries or college examination cells; university archival provenance is under audit.
  3. `UNVERIFIED`: Sourced from secondary student blogs or unofficial compilations; pending validation.
  4. `NOT_AVAILABLE`: Officially non-existent or unarchived session. Zero guessing permitted.
  5. `DEMO`: Curated model drill question clearly partitioned from real papers.
